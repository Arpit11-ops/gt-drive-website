# GT Drive Brand Palette

- GT Drive Green: `#24B34B` - Primary green used in GT letterform, section bars, bullets, icons, and emphasis text.
- Deep Green: `#087D36` - Darker green visible in logo accent/shadow and some social/graphic elements.
- Near Black: `#050505` - Primary wordmark and headline black.
- Text Black: `#1A1A1A` - Body text and labels.
- Light City Gray: `#E8E8E8` - Soft city/background artwork and light separators.
- White: `#FFFFFF` - Dominant page background.

Note: Hex values are visually sampled approximations from the brochure PDF renders, not an official brand guideline file.
