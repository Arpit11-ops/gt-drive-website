## Logo And Visual Identity Notes

- Logo style: bold italic black `GT DRIVE` wordmark with a green stylized `T`.
- Tagline style: uppercase italic text, usually placed below or top-right as `DRIVE CLEAN, GO GREEN`.
- The logo includes a small green charging/plug icon near the tagline on the cover and contact page.
- Brand layout is mostly white space with green horizontal bars and black footer bands.
- Product pages use a consistent split layout: scooter imagery on the left, specifications and features on the right.
- Background visual language: pale city/building backdrop, suggesting urban mobility.
- Icon language: simple green circular icons for eco, safety, zero pollution, and range claims.
- Bullet style: green arrow/chevron bullets.
- Typography feel: bold italic uppercase display text for model names and logo/tagline; clean sans-serif body text; feature list appears in a more italic/stylized type treatment.
