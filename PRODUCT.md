## Platform

web

## Users

The website serves two primary audiences:

- Motor dealers and distributors evaluating GT Drive as a dealership or business opportunity.
- Scooter buyers researching GT Drive electric scooter models for product information.

Dealers need to understand the company, manufacturing footprint, product range, service support, and dealership opportunity. Buyers need to browse individual scooter models, compare available brochure-confirmed details, and contact the company only for information or enquiry. The website is not intended for direct online selling to customers.

## Product Purpose

The website exists to present GT Drive as a premium electric scooter brand and provide reliable product information from the brochure. It may collect enquiries through forms, phone, WhatsApp, and email, but it must not behave like a direct-to-customer ecommerce or online sales site.

Success means visitors can:

- Understand GT Drive and Houstan Innovations LLP.
- Explore the scooter model catalogue through full product pages.
- See verified specifications, features, color options, and product status from the brochure.
- Discover the pan-India dealership opportunity.
- Contact the company through phone, WhatsApp, email, or enquiry submission for more information.

## Positioning

GT Drive is an Indian electric two-wheeler brand from Houstan Innovations LLP, built around clean mobility, in-house lithium battery capability, advanced manufacturing, a wide dealer network, and reliable service support.

The site should position GT Drive as a premium and trustworthy electric scooter brand for both business partners and scooter buyers. It should support rich product storytelling with images and videos when assets are available, without inventing technical claims not present in the brochure or confirmed by the owner. It must avoid direct-selling language such as checkout, cart, buy online, online payment, limited-time offer, or ecommerce-style purchase funnels unless the owner later confirms that business model.

- Full product pages for scooter models.
- Dealer/distributor-focused dealership opportunity section or page.
- Buyer-friendly model browsing for product information.
- Contact options including phone, WhatsApp, visible email, and enquiry submission.
- Brand presentation using GT Drive's green, black, and white identity.

Confirmed scooter models from the brochure:

- GT - SOUL (SL)

- GT - RYD (CS)

- GT - ONE PLUS (BMW)
- GT - CHAMPION (CJ)

- GT - DRIVE PRO

Constraints and open product facts:

- Do not invent price, battery capacity, certified range, top speed, charging time, motor wattage, warranty, load capacity, charger specifications, dimensions, registration category, or certifications.
- GT - CHETAK has no detailed specs in the brochure and should not inherit specs from other models without owner confirmation.
- The model code `BMW` for GT - ONE PLUS appears in the brochure but should be confirmed before prominent public use because it may be confused with the automobile brand.
- The brochure's address spelling `Hydrabad` likely means `Hyderabad`; confirm before publishing the final address copy.
- The brochure repeats both `Variable Drive Modes` and `Multiple Drive Modes`; keep both only if the owner confirms they are distinct, or normalize carefully as website copy.

## Product Principles

1. Brochure truth first: use confirmed brochure content and clearly mark missing or unclear facts.
2. Inform before selling: the site presents models, features, locations, and dealership information; it does not push direct customer purchase.
3. Product clarity: each scooter model must keep its own specs, features, colors, and status correctly grouped.
4. Premium credibility: the site should feel high-quality and brand-led, but never at the cost of false technical claims.
5. Asset discipline: use owner-provided images and videos when available; use extracted assets only as references or temporary scaffolding.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The website should be usable on mobile and desktop, with readable product information, clear contact actions, strong contrast, and no layout choices that hide key enquiry paths. No product-specific accessibility standard has been provided yet.
